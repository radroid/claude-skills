02

Every overlap claim, refuted

Five pairs scored 3 (substantial — "a user would have to choose") on the first pass. Each went to a verifier told to default to refuted and to cite both files. All five came back down to 2. The recurring reason: Pocock's skills that sound like collisions carry disable-model-invocation: true, so they can only fire on a typed slash command — they cannot contest a natural-language trigger at all.

05

Why they don't collide

The corpora are built for two different people at two different moments. Pocock's assume a human at the keyboard; this repo's assume the human has left the room. Every number below was counted from the files, not estimated.
